# Multi-Jurisdiction Compliance Response Matrix

**Lab 3 (Required)** · Innocent BUGINGO

**Company:** ShopGhana · **Scenario:** Customer data deletion requests from three countries
**Regulations:** Rwanda's Law No 058/2021 · EU GDPR · California CCPA/CPRA

> Adapted for the Rwanda cohort: Customer A is assessed under Rwanda's Law No 058/2021 instead of Ghana's Act 843.

---

## 1. Customer A – Abena (Rwanda · Law No 058/2021)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Legal Right** | Right to erasure (Art. 23) when the data is no longer needed, consent is withdrawn, or processing was unlawful. |
| **Exemptions** | Data needed for a legal obligation, legal claims, public interest, or research and statistics (Art. 23). |
| **Can Retain** | Order and payment records required by Rwanda's tax rules, with restricted access, deleted when that period ends. |
| **Response Deadline** | **30 days** from receiving the request (Art. 23). If she is unhappy, she can appeal to the NCSA within 30 days. |
| **Penalties** | Administrative fines of RWF 2–5 million or 1% of turnover (Art. 53); unlawful processing offences up to 5% of annual turnover (Art. 62). |
| **Action Steps** | 1. Verify her identity.<br>2. Delete her profile, addresses and marketing data.<br>3. Tell any third party holding her data to erase it (Art. 23).<br>4. Keep only legally required records.<br>5. Reply in writing and log the request. |
| **Draft Response** | *"Dear Abena, we have deleted your account and personal data as you requested. We keep only your payment records, which Rwandan tax law requires, and will delete them when that period ends. If you are not satisfied, you may appeal to the National Cyber Security Authority."* |

## 2. Customer B – Lukas (Germany · GDPR)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Legal Right** | Strong right to erasure, the "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17). |
| **Exemptions** | Legal obligations such as tax records, and the defence of legal claims (Art. 17(3)). |
| **Can Retain** | Invoices and transaction records required by tax law, nothing else. |
| **Response Deadline** | **1 month**, extendable by 2 more months for complex cases if he is told why (Art. 12(3)). |
| **Penalties** | Up to **€20 million or 4% of global annual turnover**, whichever is higher (Art. 83). |
| **Action Steps** | 1. Verify his identity.<br>2. Erase his data from all systems and backups.<br>3. Notify every processor and partner that received it (Art. 19).<br>4. Keep only tax records.<br>5. Confirm in writing within one month. |
| **Draft Response** | *"Dear Lukas, under GDPR Article 17 we have erased your personal data and informed our partners to do the same. We keep only invoices that tax law requires us to hold."* |

## 3. Customer C – Maria (California · CCPA/CPRA)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Legal Right** | Right to delete, and right to opt out of the sale or sharing of her data. |
| **Can Deletion Be Immediate?** | **No.** She has an active return dispute, and the CCPA allows keeping data needed to complete a transaction. |
| **Can Retain** | Order and dispute records until the return is resolved. |
| **Response Deadline** | **45 days**, extendable once by 45 more (90 total) with notice. The opt-out must be honoured right away. |
| **Penalties** | Up to $2,500 per violation, or $7,500 if intentional (inflation-adjusted since 2025). |
| **Action Steps** | 1. Verify her identity.<br>2. Stop selling or sharing her data immediately.<br>3. Keep only dispute-related records.<br>4. Delete everything once the dispute closes.<br>5. Tell her both steps in writing. |
| **Draft Response** | *"Dear Maria, we have stopped selling or sharing your personal information. We will delete your data once your return dispute is resolved, as the law allows us to keep it until then."* |

## 4. Compliance Comparison Table

| Element | Rwanda (Law 058/2021) | GDPR | CCPA/CPRA |
|---------|----------------------|------|-----------|
| **Right to deletion?** | Yes, on listed grounds | Yes, strong | Yes |
| **Main exemptions** | Legal obligation, legal claims, public interest, research | Legal obligation, legal claims | Completing a transaction, legal obligation |
| **Response deadline** | 30 days | 1 month (+2) | 45 days (+45) |
| **Penalties** | 1% turnover (admin), 5% (offences) | €20M or 4% of global turnover | $2,500 / $7,500 per violation |
| **Consent model** | Consent or other lawful basis, withdrawable anytime | Explicit, informed consent | Opt-out of sale or sharing |

## 5. Key Insights

- **GDPR** has the highest fines and the strictest duty to notify partners.
- **Rwanda's law** is close to GDPR, with a firm 30-day reply and an appeal to the NCSA.
- **CCPA/CPRA** focuses on opt-out control and allows delays for open transactions.
- One shared process (verify identity, check exemptions, delete, notify third parties, reply in writing) works for all three, with local deadlines.
